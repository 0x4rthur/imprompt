# Imprompt 0.1.7

- A new look everywhere: soft, raised cards on a light gray canvas (or deep ink at
  night), a floating graphite menu and big, clear headlines. Same Imprompt colors,
  with the ink as the only accent.
- Home answers "is it working?" at a glance: a status headline, your shortcut,
  quick tiles for API, preset, output and theme, your last imprompt next to its
  result, and this month's tokens, spending and imprompts.
- Motion with a purpose: the selection slides to where you click, tabs cascade in
  from the direction you moved, numbers count up and the popup grows the result
  card to fit the text. "Reduce motion" in Windows turns it all into direct changes.
- Popup: the Enter action is always the dark button, errors shake once, Copy
  confirms, and a preset picked by number scrolls into view.
- The "Imprompting…" window animates the [I] mark: the brackets open, the I reads
  the line, and they close again.
- History, Presets, API, Shortcut and Settings rebuilt as cards; the default preset
  and the provider are tiles with a ring that slides to your choice.
- Layout holds down to the smallest window size.

Update from **Settings → Updates → Download and restart**, or run
**Imprompt_0.1.7_x64-setup.exe**.

Validation: frontend tests (70) and TypeScript, Rust fmt/clippy/tests, production
build, and a visual pass of every screen in both themes and both languages.
