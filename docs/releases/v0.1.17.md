# Imprompt 0.1.17

The popup now understands a preset change even when you keep pressing Enter at
a normal human pace.

- 0.1.16 only recognised Enter pressed many times when the presses were less
  than 0.3 s apart. People press every 0.3 to 0.5 s, so each press still counted
  as a confirmation: after you switched presets the new result was pasted as
  soon as it showed up, and the popup could close between two presses. The
  window is now 0.6 s.
- After you switch presets, pressing Enter repeatedly only generates with the
  new preset. The result stays in the popup until you stop for a moment and
  press Enter once more.
- The result card now names the preset: "Generating · <preset>" while it works
  and "Result · <preset>" when it is done, so you can see that the change was
  understood. The footer says "new preset · Enter applies it" while it waits.
- On the same preset, Enter pressed repeatedly still pastes, but only once you
  have stopped pressing for 0.6 s, so no Enter lands in the app underneath.

Update from **Settings → Updates → Download and restart**, or run
**Imprompt_0.1.17_x64-setup.exe**.

Validation: frontend tests (124) and TypeScript, Rust fmt/clippy/tests (109),
production build, and the sequences above replayed with real keys in a browser
at a human pace (one Enter every 0.3 s) against a simulated API.
